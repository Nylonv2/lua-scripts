Take photos of the Delivery Notifications screen, on the SKU Summary tab. Scroll and shoot until every row is covered, then tap “Scan a delivery” and pick them: several at once, or one at a time with “Add more photos” (up to 12). Overlapping photos are fine. Claude reads the rows and builds your room plan.

Already have the delivery as text? Tap “Paste a list”.

How the counts are read. The two “Q…” columns are taken as units arriving, then units already in the store. Rows marked “Check count” had a number the scan could not read.

Shoe photos

Needs the most room

    “Find shoe photos” has Claude search the web for each shoe and saves the product picture it finds; pictures found that way are marked “AI” and can be wrong, so replace or remove any that don't match. Tap a shoe drawing with a “+” to add your own photo of that shoe. Tap a photo to see it larger, replace it or remove it. “See photos” opens an image search for that shoe. Tick a card once you have made room. “…” marks text that was cut off on the screen. Everything is saved on this phone only.

    Photos are read with your own Claude key. Get one at console.anthropic.com → API keys. The key stays on this phone and is sent only to Anthropic. Each scan costs a few cents; finding shoe photos uses web search and costs roughly 1–3 cents a shoe.
