    What she's allowed to do

    Texts: she writes the message in your texting app and you tap Send, so nothing goes out without you. Your notifications are read on the phone only; nothing is saved or uploaded.

    Hands-free: “Hey …”

    Like Siri: say “Hey” and her name, then ask anything (“Hey Rias, set a timer for 10 minutes”), or just “Hey Rias” and she'll say hello and wait for your question. A short buzz tells you she heard you.

    Listening happens on your phone with an offline speech model (a one-time 40 MB download); nothing leaves the phone until you say the wake word. No beeps while it waits. Android requires a small silent “listening” notification while the microphone is on (on Android 13+ you can swipe it away). A one-word name that's easy to hear works best.
